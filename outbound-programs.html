What Is OutBound Training?

Immersive, Off-Site Experiences that Build High-Trust, High-Performance Teams.

Outbound Training is a power-packed, experiential learning model where corporate teams step out of the office and into nature-driven, challenge-based environments — to unlock their full leadership, communication, collaboration, and behavioral potential.

Designed around the psychology of experience and the power of group dynamics, this training allows teams to break silos, face fears, align values, and operate with unity, trust, and confidence.
This isn’t team bonding.

It’s team transformation — driven by breakthrough moments, high-energy challenges, and deep reflection.

The GAD Experiential Performance Model™ – 7-Phase Outbound Journey

A Proven Framework for Transformative Outbound Training

Our Outbound Training is built on the GAD Experiential Performance Model™, a 7-phase structure that ensures the program delivers measurable shifts, not just adrenaline.

1

Orientation & Behavioral Baseline Mapping

We start by understanding your team — its goals, gaps, and behavioral challenges.

Outcome: Team Awareness | Baseline Insights | Goal Clarity
2

Intent Setting & Role Awareness

We align each member to their role, team dynamics, and transformation goals.

Outcome: Role Alignment | Shared Vision | Team Cohesion
3

Challenge-Based Activities & Tactical Missions

Teams engage in purpose-designed outdoor simulations that test communication, strategy, agility, and leadership.

Outcome: Problem-Solving | Collaboration | Adaptive Leadership
4

Reflective Labs & Debrief Circles

Each activity is followed by powerful reflection sessions led by trained facilitators, ensuring every experience becomes a breakthrough.

Outcome: Deeper Insights | Behavioral Shifts | Collective Learning
5

Real-Time Coaching & Feedback Interventions

Leaders and team members receive targeted coaching on behavior, mindset, influence, and group dynamics.

Outcome: Growth Mindset | Stronger Influence | Improved Team Dynamics
6

Integration Design & Action Planning

We extract key takeaways and build behavioral charters and next-step commitments for post-training application.

Outcome: Practical Action Plans | Accountability | Sustained Change
7

Post-Training Impact Tracker & Culture Reinforcement

Optional: We follow up with the team weeks later to measure implementation and continue the behavior shift.

Outcome: Lasting Impact | Culture Reinforcement | Continuous Growth
