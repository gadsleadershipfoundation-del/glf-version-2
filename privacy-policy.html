Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how GAD’s Leadership Foundation (“GLF”) collects, uses, and protects information provided by users on www.geraldarundass.com, participants in coaching programs, workshop attendees, and consulting clients.

Information We Collect

We collect both personal and non-personal information via forms, assessments, payment gateways, and website analytics (cookies, IPs, usage behavior). This includes:

  • • Name, email, phone number, address
  • • Payment details, assessments, feedback forms
  • • Browsing history, device information (via cookies)

How We Use the Data

  • • To provide services (coaching, workshops, consulting)
  • • To customize your experience and recommend services
  • • For administrative, contractual, or legal compliance
  • • For marketing, retargeting, and testimonials (see clause below)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data is shared only with:

  • • Authorized service providers
  • • Cloud storage/CRM/email vendors under confidentiality
  • • Legal bodies if required

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and pixel tags to improve our website experience and analyze traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser.

Data Protection

Your data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

This site is not intended for children under 13. No data is knowingly collected from minors.

You can choose not to provide certain information, but then you might not be able to take advantage of many of our features. No refund shall be given to any participant who is registering and being a part of our programs. GLF uses reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your Personal Information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction, taking into due account the risks involved in the processing undertaken and the nature of the Personal Information we collect. We will keep your personal data for as long as we need it, or as otherwise prescribed by law, for the purposes set out above. This period will vary depending on your interactions with us. We may also keep a record of correspondence with you (for example if you have made a complaint about a product) for as long as is necessary to protect us from a legal claim.
